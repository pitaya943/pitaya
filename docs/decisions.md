| D59 | 2026-11-20 | Threat model, decided after the review of 0.1.4. pitaya assumes cooperating agents. It defends, in this order, against (1) harness logic errors: rules, skills or workflows that contradict each other, deadlock, miscount, or claim to prove something they don't; (2) honest agent mistakes, with cheap guards: loosening a test or a check to get green, editing CI, verifying its own work, editing a verdict, "fixing" a merge with a strategy that drops changes; (3) untrusted external input, strictly: issue and PR text from anyone, and upstream code. Deliberate circumvention by the agent itself (forging a verdict, rewriting the workflow that judges it, getting round a hook, forging a commit email) is out of scope for now: the trust assumption is documented where it applies, and no defence is built. Level 3 therefore means "agents I trust"; hooks prevent accidents and are not a security boundary. The tripwire is a D60 Attention row of kind `undisclosed`: when one appears, the Owner decides whether to bring back the defences the review lists | Every problem the trial found was a logic error or an honest mistake; defences against a hostile agent cost more than the trial justifies, and the review's list stays as what to bring back if the assumption fails | - |
